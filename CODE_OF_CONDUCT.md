# Code of conduct

This code applies to every repository under [github.com/uniqaiapp](https://github.com/uniqaiapp) that does not ship its own, and to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space those repositories run.

## Expected

- Be direct and be civil. Disagree with the code, the data or the wording, not with the person.
- Assume the other side is acting in good faith; ask before concluding otherwise.
- Keep contributions honest: no invented numbers, no fabricated sources, no content you do not have the right to share.
- Respect the maintainers' time and the contributors' time: small, reproducible reports beat long ones.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, slurs or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, vote-brigading, star-exchange schemes or advertising unrelated products.
- Sustained disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete content and may block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem to **support@uniqai.app**; reports are handled privately. Decisions are made by the UniqAI maintainers and are final within these repositories.
